Fortune Wiki

Every fortune card is built from four parts: a Magic Stone, a Power, an Order, and a Class. Deck battles resolve card-to-card — Card 1 vs Card 1, Card 2 vs Card 2, Card 3 vs Card 3. Click any fortune below to read what it does.

Battle Rules

Magic Stone

Can completely eradicate an opposing card in the same lane, depending on rarity. A stronger stone deletes the rival before damage is even rolled.

Power

The most determining, numerical force on a card. Power damage is the main destruction potential when stones do not wipe the lane clean.

Order

Grants protection health. Values range from 0 to infinite depending on the order — higher rarity orders hold the line far longer.

Class

Applies an effect before battle begins — either a debuff on the opposing deck or a buff on your own. The only fortune that can influence more than one card. Reach scales with rarity — for example, a rare Class on Card 1 can affect Cards 1, 2 & 3.

Magic Stones

Eradication strength rises with rarity. Common stones only delete fragile rivals; Impossible stones erase any lane opponent outright.

Common

Spadow

A dim shadow-cut gem. In its lane (Card N vs Card N), Spadow can eradicate an opposing card only if that card’s Magic Stone is also common — otherwise the rival stays standing and Power decides the fight.

Sphynx

Whispering riddle stone. Eradicates the lane rival when their stone rarity is common and their Order protection is under 40. If the puzzle fails, nothing is deleted and battle continues normally.

Cornelious

Grainy amber shard. Completely eradicates a same-lane opponent whose stone is common, then softens leftover Order health by a small amount if eradication somehow fails.

Abraham

Old covenant stone. Eradicates the opposing lane card only on a clean rarity edge — common vs common. Against rare or higher stones it does nothing special and Power takes over.

Dragsten

Smoky dragon-scale crystal. Can eradicate a common-stone rival in the same lane after a short delay, which means Class debuffs still apply first before the wipe lands.

Sparklows

Flicker-bright pebble. Eradicates the opposing card if that card’s combined rarity score is low (mostly commons). High-tier rivals shrug it off and trade Power damage instead.

Amerix

Common-cut Amerix. Same name as its rare twin, but weaker: eradicates only common-stone lane rivals and never touches rare or ultra rare stones.

Cardoush

Paper-thin slate. Eradicates a same-lane card by shredding its presence when the rival stone is common. Against thicker stones it leaves a paper cut — no full wipe.

Yoklow

Mud-glow ore. Completely eradicates common-stone opponents in the matching lane; if the rival survives, Yoklow does not alter Power or Order — pure erase-or-nothing.

Jaminix

Jittery blue chip. Has a chance to eradicate a common-stone lane rival before Power resolves. Failure simply means a normal damage-versus-protection clash.

Rare

Pendragon

Crown-flame jewel. Eradicates any opposing card in the same lane whose Magic Stone is common or rare. Ultra rare and Impossible stones still resist the purge.

Amerix

Rare-cut Amerix. Sharper than the common version: eradicates common and rare lane rivals, and halves the protection of an ultra rare rival if eradication is blocked.

Glownatrax

Neon lattice stone. Floods the lane with light and eradicates common or rare opposing stones on contact. Surviving ultra rares fight through the glare at full Power.

Galliardo

Duelist’s gem. Eradicates the matching-lane rival if their stone rarity is lower or equal (common/rare). The wipe happens after Class debuffs, so softened targets fall first.

Ultra Rare

Spanxlow

Horizon-tear crystal. Completely eradicates any opposing card in the same lane unless that card holds the Impossible stone Wuyowa. Power and Order never get a say once Spanxlow lands.

Impossible??!

Wuyowa

The stone that should not exist. Eradicates the opposing lane card no matter its rarity — including Spanxlow — before Class, Power, or Order resolve. Nothing in a deck battle outranks Wuyowa’s wipe.

Powers

Power is the most determining number in a contested lane. Damage chews through Order protection after stones fail to eradicate.

Common

Shadow40 damage

Soft umbral strike. Deals 40 damage to the opposing card’s Order protection in the same lane. Best for finishing rivals already weakened by Class debuffs; alone it rarely cracks rare Orders.

Sponk50 damage

Blunt cartoon punch. Deals 50 damage — the strongest common Power. Reliable against fragile common Orders (under 50 protection) and a steady chip against rarer shields.

Drawg13 damage

Slow drag scrape. Only 13 damage, the weakest Power in the machine. Needs Class debuffs or a stone eradication to matter; otherwise it barely scratches most Orders.

Rare

Grapple67 damage

Latch-and-crush force. Deals 67 damage and tends to lock the lane duel so the rival cannot “slip” — destruction potential is solid mid-tier against common and light rare Orders.

Casticize80 damage

Purifying bolt. Deals 80 damage, strong enough to shatter most common protection values in one hit and dent rare Orders hard before a second exchange.

Galvazprinx100 damage

Charged prism blast. A clean 100 damage — the numerical pivot of many rare fights. Destroys common Orders outright and threatens mid-rare protection in a single strike.

Gluon Array200 damage

Particle lattice barrage. Deals 200 damage, the hardest rare Power. Cracks every common Order and most rare Orders; only ultra rare protection still stands tall after one hit.

Sparker Grind156 damage

Grinding spark mill. Deals 156 damage with lingering burn feel — excellent destruction potential that shreds common shields and leaves rare Orders barely clinging.

Ultra Rare

Atom Bomb123049 damage

Cataclysm number. Deals 123049 damage — enough to vaporize every finite Order protection value in the wiki. Only Infinite protection (Sp4ti4l 4n0m4ly) can theoretically survive the blast.

Ant Army1034 damage

Swarm of tiny bites that sum to 1034 damage. Overwhelms common and rare Orders easily and pressures ultra rare shields that are not near-infinite.

Crystal Bomb670 damage

Shrapnel detonation for 670 damage. Destroys all common and rare Orders in one hit and deeply wounds ultra rare protection short of Ultimum / Sp4ti4l 4n0m4ly.

Orders

Orders give protection health that absorbs Power damage. Values range from 0 to Infinite — click each order for its exact shield.

Common

Smiths35 protection

Workshop guild shield. Grants 35 protection health — enough to soak Shadow or Drawg once, but Sponk and any rare Power smash straight through.

Sphynxs48 protection

Riddle-bound ward. Grants 48 protection. Survives Shadow with a sliver left; Sponk (50) and everything rarer still break it in one hit.

Shallows20 protection

Thin coastal barrier. Only 20 protection — the second-weakest common Order. Almost any Power deletes it unless Class debuffs never get a chance to matter.

Gravestones Union55 protection

Cemetery collective armor. Grants 55 protection, tankiest among commons. Blocks all common Powers once; rare Powers still punch through.

Galeccia Family42 protection

House crest barrier. Grants 42 protection — stops Shadow, fails against Sponk and every higher Power without help from stone eradication or Class debuffs.

Cascadia30 protection

Rain-curtain ward. Grants 30 protection. Soft common shield that only Drawg fails to clear; Shadow and above crack it open.

Klorence Army50 protection

Rank-and-file plating. Grants 50 protection — exactly matches Sponk, so common-vs-common often ends in a mutual break unless a stone eradicates first.

Vegetables0 protection

Salad solidarity. Grants 0 protection — the floor of Order health. Any Power destroys the card immediately if the Magic Stone did not already eradicate the rival.

Caned25 protection

Woven cane shell. Grants 25 protection. Slightly tougher than Shallows, still erased by every Power except Drawg.

Rare

Sparxiax Galactic180 protection

Starfleet lattice. Grants 180 protection. Absorbs every common Power and most rare Powers; Gluon Array (200) is the rare strike that still breaks it cold.

Cullmanstox220 protection

Industrial vault plating. Grants 220 protection — survives all rare Powers except a fresh Gluon Array is close; ultra rare Powers still crush it.

Imperial Family300 protection

Bloodline bastion. Grants 300 protection. Holds against every rare Power including Gluon Array; only ultra rare destruction numbers reliably end the line.

Clarxmaidens150 protection

Choir-guard veil. Grants 150 protection. Stops commons and lighter rares; Sparker Grind (156) and Gluon Array (200) still tear it down.

Ultra Rare

Royal Family800 protection

Throne-grade aegis. Grants 800 protection — shrugs off Crystal Bomb’s first bite if debuffed down, but Ant Army and Atom Bomb still overwhelm the crown.

Ultimum5000 protection

Final-wall doctrine. Grants 5000 protection. Survives Crystal Bomb and Ant Army with room to spare; only Atom Bomb’s absurd number can shatter Ultimum outright.

Glass'o Corp.1200 protection

Corporate mirror shield. Grants 1200 protection. Crystal Bomb chips it; Ant Army cracks it; Atom Bomb deletes the quarterly report.

Sp4ti4l 4n0m4lyInfinite protection

Broken geometry Order. Grants Infinite protection — Power damage cannot reduce it. The only ways past are Magic Stone eradication or Class debuffs that strip the anomaly before the clash.

Classes

About half of Classes buff your own deck; the rest debuff the opposing deck. Common affects 1 card (same lane). Rare can influence Cards 1, 2 & 3. Ultra rare pushes the whole deck harder.

Common affects 1 card

Technologist

Debuff: Short Circuit — reduces the opposing same-lane card’s Power damage by 20% before battle. Only affects that one rival card.

Spiritualist

Buff: Blessing Veil — raises your same-lane card’s Order protection by 20 before Power resolves. Lane-only self support.

Farmer

Buff: Harvest Surge — adds +15 Power damage to your same-lane card for this clash, grown from steady prep before battle.

Explorer

Debuff: Mapped Weakness — reveals and marks the rival lane card so your Power ignores 10 of their protection. Affects Card N vs Card N only.

Settler

Buff: Fortify Claim — reinforces your same-lane Order with +25 protection and lets your Magic Stone attempt eradication one beat earlier.

Scientist

Buff: Field Notes — calibrates your same-lane Power for +12 damage and +8 protection this battle only.

Fugitive

Debuff: Smoke Trail — the opposing same-lane card’s Class effect is cancelled this round, then they take a flat −5 protection.

Funeralist

Debuff: Last Rites — if the opposing same-lane card’s Order is already under 40 protection, they lose all remaining protection before Power hits.

Healer

Buff: Mend — restores +30 Order protection to your same-lane card before the clash. Vegetables jump from 0 to a real shield for one fight.

Warrior

Buff: War Cry — pumps your same-lane Power by +18 damage before the trade. Pure self aggression, no rival touch.

Soldier

Debuff: Suppressive Fire — the opposing same-lane card deals half Power this round if their Power rarity is common; otherwise −10 damage.

Criminal

Debuff: Pickpocket Protocol — steals a temporary −1 rarity step from the opposing same-lane Magic Stone for eradication checks this battle only.

Rare affects up to 3 cards

Office Clerk

Debuff: Paperwork Flood — when played on Card 1, floods opposing Cards 1, 2 & 3 with forms that each lose 20 Power damage before battle. From Card 2 or 3, still hits the whole opposing deck.

Medic

Buff: Triage Network — heals +40 Order protection across your Cards 1, 2 & 3 before any lane resolves.

Governor

Debuff: Martial Decree — locks stone eradication on opposing Cards 1–3 for one beat, forcing Power-vs-Order fights deck-wide.

Ally

Buff: Shared Supply — each of your Cards 1–3 gains +15 Power damage and +20 protection before the clash.

Leader

Buff: Rally Call — inspires your Cards 1, 2 & 3 so each deals +25 Power damage this battle.

Landlord

Debuff: Eviction Notice — strips 40 protection from every opposing card (1–3). Vegetables and other low Orders often fold before Power even swings.

Conquistador

Debuff: Claimed Territory — opposing Cards 1–3 cannot use Class effects this round, and each loses 15 protection.

Mastermind

Debuff: Triple Scheme — predicts the fight: opposing Cards 1–3 each suffer −15% Power and −15 protection before lanes resolve.

Specialist

Buff: Tuned Doctrine — your same-lane Magic Stone gains +1 effective rarity for eradication, and your other two cards each gain +35 Power.

Cyborg

Buff: Signal Boost — overclocks your Cards 1–3 for +30% Power and +25 protection each this battle.

Ultra Rare deck-wide pressure

Monopolist

Debuff: Market Collapse — hits opposing Cards 1, 2 & 3 for −50% Power and −100 protection each before battle. The harshest economy wipe short of erasing stones.

Agent

Debuff: Black File — marks the whole opposing deck: Cards 1–3 lose stone eradication rights this round and take +80 incoming Power damage apiece.

World Leader

Buff: Global Mandate — your Cards 1–3 each gain +50% Power and +200 protection (Infinite Orders stay Infinite). Full-deck self empowerment.

Star Fighter

Buff: Orbital Escort — shields your Cards 1–3 with +150 protection each and lets every friendly Magic Stone attempt eradication one rarity step higher this round.
