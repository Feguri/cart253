Fortune Wiki

Every fortune card is built from four parts: a Magic Stone, a Power, an Order, and a Class. Deck battles resolve card-to-card — Card 1 vs Card 1, Card 2 vs Card 2, Card 3 vs Card 3.

Battle Rules Magic Stones Powers Orders Classes Rarity & Tiers

Battle Rules

Magic Stone

Can completely eradicate an opposing card in the same lane, depending on rarity. A stronger stone deletes the rival before damage is even rolled.

Power

The most determining, numerical force on a card. Power damage is the main destruction potential when stones do not wipe the lane clean.

Order

Grants protection health. Values range from 0 to infinite depending on the order — higher rarity orders hold the line far longer.

Class

Applies debuffs to opposing cards before battle begins. The only fortune that can influence more than one rival card. Reach scales with rarity — for example, a rare Class on Card 1 can hit Cards 1, 2 & 3 of the opposing deck.
